# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery if the item is damaged, defective, incorrect, or never arrived.

Customers must provide an order ID when requesting a refund.

Refunds usually take 5 to 10 business days to appear on the original payment method.

Refunds may be rejected if the order is older than 30 days or if the item was damaged after delivery.
